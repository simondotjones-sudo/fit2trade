Modern Slavery & Human Trafficking Statement

Our position on forced labour, human trafficking and responsible treatment of people in Fit2Trade business relationships.

Last updated19 August 2026 OrganisationFit2Trade Ltd
On this page Our position Our people Suppliers and business partners Responsible business expectations Reporting a concern Review responsibility
01

Our position

Fit2Trade opposes modern slavery, human trafficking, forced labour and the exploitation of workers. We expect our business activities and relationships to be conducted lawfully and with respect for human rights and human dignity.

This is a voluntary corporate statement of Fit2Trade’s position and expectations. It does not state or imply that Fit2Trade is required to publish a statement under any particular modern-slavery statute.

02

Our people

People working for Fit2Trade are expected to follow applicable law and our ethical standards, treat colleagues and business contacts with respect, and raise concerns where they become aware of conduct that may involve exploitation, coercion or other serious labour-rights issues.

03

Suppliers and business partners

Our Partner Code of Conduct states that suppliers and partners must not use forced, bonded, trafficked or child labour and should maintain lawful and fair employment practices.

We expect concerns relevant to modern slavery or human trafficking in a Fit2Trade business relationship to be taken seriously and brought to our attention.

04

Responsible business expectations

Fit2Trade’s approach is based on clear standards rather than unsupported claims about audit programmes or supply-chain statistics. We expect lawful conduct, fair treatment, safe working conditions, responsible handling of concerns and appropriate action where credible issues are identified.

05

Reporting a concern

Employees, suppliers, contractors, partners or other people who have a concern connected with Fit2Trade can contact concerns@fit2trade.com.

If a concern suggests immediate danger or criminal activity, the appropriate emergency service or competent authority should also be contacted where necessary.

06

Review responsibility

Fit2Trade management is responsible for keeping this statement under review as the company, its supplier relationships and relevant legal requirements develop.
