Learning & MCC

Make qualification plans and required learning easier to see and manage.

The Central Bank’s expanded knowledge and competence standards took effect for credit unions on 1 October 2024, with transitional arrangements running to 1 October 2028. Fit2Trade can help teams keep relevant roles, recognised qualifications, transition plans, required learning and supporting records visible in one place.

A dedicated readiness pathway sits alongside the wider Credit Union solution.
