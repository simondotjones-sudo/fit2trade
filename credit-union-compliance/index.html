MCC transition

Make the 2028 qualification deadline visible now.

The Central Bank’s expanded knowledge and competence standards took effect for credit unions on 1 October 2024, with transitional arrangements running to 1 October 2028. Fit2Trade helps teams document who is in scope, the relevant qualification plan and the evidence behind it.

Operational resilience

Turn preparation into owned, recurring work.

The Central Bank has stated that DORA will apply to Irish credit unions from January 2028. Fit2Trade readiness packs structure policies, ICT risks, vendor oversight, incidents, testing and board reporting into visible actions and evidence.
