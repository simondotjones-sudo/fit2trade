Company

Technology for the work that happens across people, sites and frontline teams.

Fit2Trade builds connected software for organisations managing learning, safety, compliance and day-to-day operational standards. The aim is simple: make the work clearer for frontline teams and more visible to the people responsible for it.

What we focus on

Start with how the organisation actually works.

Frontline systems work best when they reflect real roles, locations and responsibilities. Fit2Trade uses one shared organisational core so learning, safety, operational checks, communications, licences and contractor compliance can connect without becoming one oversized system.

That shared foundation matters because the same people and sites often sit behind several different workflows. Keeping the structure together reduces duplication and makes it easier to see what needs attention.
