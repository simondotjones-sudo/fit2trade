This redesigned page presents the substance of Fit2Trade’s existing published policy in a clearer format. Contract-specific terms continue to apply where agreed separately.

01

Opening statement

Fit2Trade takes privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, store, use and disclose information when you use our websites, related marketing activities or Fit2Trade services. Fit2Trade Ltd, The Masonry, Hanover Quay, Dublin 2, D02 VY79, Ireland is the data controller for the purposes described here.

02

Information you provide

We may collect information you choose to provide through forms or correspondence. When services are supplied, this can include names, email addresses, work location, course or activity information and other information uploaded in connection with the service.

03

Information collected automatically

When you use our websites or services, technical and usage information may be recorded, including device or browser information, IP address, referring pages, pages visited, timestamps, service activity and performance information.

04

How information is used

Information may be used to operate and improve the website and services, administer accounts, complete requested transactions, respond to enquiries, provide technical and administrative communications, analyse usage, protect the service and communicate about relevant products where permitted by your preferences.

05

Sharing and disclosure

Fit2Trade does not rent or sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers acting on our behalf, as part of a business transfer, or when reasonably necessary to meet legal obligations, enforce agreements or protect rights, property and safety.

06

Security

Fit2Trade uses technical, organisational and administrative measures intended to protect information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. No system can guarantee complete security, and users are responsible for protecting their credentials.

07

Cookies and analytics

The website and some services may use cookies and similar technologies to support navigation, functionality and analysis. See the Cookie Policy for further information about categories and choices.

08

Your rights

Applicable data-protection law may provide rights to information, access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. To exercise a right or ask a privacy question, contact info@fit2trade.com.

09

Customer accounts

Where an employer or other organisation provides access to Fit2Trade, authorised administrators may be able to view account, learning, completion or operational information according to their role and the service configuration.

10

Contact

Questions about this policy or personal information can be sent to info@fit2trade.com or Fit2Trade Ltd, The Masonry, Hanover Quay, Dublin 2, D02 VY79, Ireland.
