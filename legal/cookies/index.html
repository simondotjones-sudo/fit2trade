What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by a browser. Similar technologies may also be used to recognise a browser, remember choices, provide functionality and understand how a website is used.

02

Essential cookies

Some cookies are necessary for core website or service functionality, security and navigation. Disabling them may prevent parts of the website or service from working correctly.

03

Analytics cookies

Analytics technologies may be used to understand visits, pages viewed, navigation and general website performance. Where consent is required, these technologies should only operate after that choice is made.

04

Preference and functional cookies

These cookies can remember settings or choices and support enhanced website functionality.

05

Managing cookies

You can manage cookies through browser controls and any consent controls presented on the website. Removing or blocking cookies may affect saved preferences or functionality.
