Ethics Statement

The principles we expect to guide responsible, lawful and fair business conduct at Fit2Trade.

Last updated19 August 2026 OrganisationFit2Trade Ltd
On this page Purpose and scope Integrity and lawful conduct Bribery, corruption and fair dealing Conflicts of interest Respectful behaviour Confidentiality, privacy and information Health, safety and responsible decisions Raising concerns Responsibility and review
01

Purpose and scope

This Ethics Statement sets out the principles Fit2Trade expects to guide business conduct. It applies to our work with colleagues, customers, suppliers, contractors and other business partners.

It is intended to support sound judgement and responsible behaviour. It does not replace applicable law, employment obligations or specific contractual requirements.

02

Integrity and lawful conduct

We aim to act honestly, fairly and in good faith. We expect people acting for Fit2Trade to comply with applicable law, communicate accurately, avoid misleading conduct and treat business commitments seriously.

03

Bribery, corruption and fair dealing

Fit2Trade does not tolerate bribery, corruption, fraud or improper inducements. Gifts, hospitality or other benefits must not be offered or accepted where they are intended, or could reasonably appear intended, to improperly influence a business decision.

We expect fair dealing with customers, competitors, suppliers and partners and do not support dishonest or anti-competitive conduct.

04

Conflicts of interest

Actual or potential conflicts between personal interests and Fit2Trade responsibilities should be disclosed so they can be considered appropriately. Business decisions should be made in the interests of Fit2Trade and the relevant customer or stakeholder obligations, not for undisclosed personal benefit.

05

Respectful behaviour

Fit2Trade expects a professional and respectful working environment. Discrimination, harassment, bullying, intimidation or other abusive conduct is not consistent with our standards.

We respect internationally recognised human rights and expect fair treatment of workers in our own operations and business relationships.

06

Confidentiality, privacy and information

Confidential information, personal data and intellectual property should be handled responsibly and used only for legitimate business purposes. Access should be limited to people who need it, and legal, contractual and security requirements should be followed.

07

Health, safety and responsible decisions

We expect people working for and with Fit2Trade to take health and safety seriously, follow relevant workplace arrangements and raise concerns where they see avoidable risk or inappropriate conduct.

08

Raising concerns

Concerns about unethical, unlawful or inappropriate conduct connected with Fit2Trade can be raised at concerns@fit2trade.com. Reports should be made in good faith and include enough information for the concern to be understood.

Fit2Trade will handle concerns responsibly and limit disclosure to those who need the information to assess or respond to the matter, subject to legal and operational requirements.

09

Responsibility and review

Fit2Trade management is responsible for keeping this statement under review and for considering whether supporting policies, training or controls need to change as the business develops.
