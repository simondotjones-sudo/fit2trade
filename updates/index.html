Entry - New Permit to Work module

What is Entry?

Entry enables clients to Manage the Permit to Work requirements for Contractors.

1. Add all your Contractor Companies

2. Set document requirements for Contractor Companies to upload, for example,

a. Insurance Certificates: Proof of in-date and adequate Public Liability and Employers' Liability Insurance.

b. Risk Assessment and Method Statement (RAMS): A site and task-specific breakdown detailing the hazards, step-by-step procedures, and hazard controls

c. Safety Statement: The contractor's statutory safety document outlining their overarching safety policies

3. Set the Competency Certifications that the employee of a Contactor must have in place, for example,

a. Manual Handling Certification: Mandatory for any worker involved in lifting, putting down, pushing, pulling, or carrying loads.

b. Safe Pass: Proves the contractor understands basic site safety, hazards, and Irish health and safety legislation.

c. Working at Height Training: Verifiable certificates for all operatives showing they have completed recognized height safety training.

d. Abrasive Wheels/Welding Certification: Proof of training for operators using cutting discs, torches, or welding equipment.

How does it work?

Client Admin Creates Contracting Companies in Entry

Sets the Document Requirements for each Contracting Company

Adds an Admin for each Contracting Company

Determines whether Contractor Induction course is required

Determines which other Certifications are required by individuals coming on sites

Contracting Company Admin

Uploads required documents to ensure their Company is compliant.

Adds their employees and the Certs required / Courses required to complete. Can view Permit to Work status of all their employees

Contracting Company Employee

Uploads any required Certifications that they have already, for example,

Safe Pass

Working at Height

Completes any course(s) assigned to them, for example;

Contractor Induction

Manual Handling

Can display their 'Permit to Work' on their mobile device when they arrive on site.

6 Nov 2025LearnEnsureAPIAccount

Critical Control Verifications (CCVs)

Questions now have the functionality to be flagged as a "Critical Control Verification" question.

View release details

Ensure

Routines › Critical Control Verifications

Questions now have the functionality to be flagged as a "Critical Control Verification" question.

A Critical Control Verification is a documented check that confirms a key risk-control measure (one that prevents a serious incident) is in place and working effectively.

Explainer:

It’s not a routine “is the floor clean?” check.

It’s a life-saving barrier check for things like fire protection, fuel containment, electrical isolation, or thermal hazards. CCV = “Are the things that keep people alive or uninjured definitely working as intended?”

If a CCV question is answered in the negative, it automatically creates a corrective action; flags it as High Priority, a Hazard, and guides that it should be addressed within 7 days. This is then visible in Corrective Actions.

These KPIs will follow:

% Critical Control Verifications Completed on Time

% Critical Controls Failed Verification

Average Time to Close Failed CCV Days from fail → verified fixed ≤ 7 days

Incidents and Accidents

Ensure > I&A > Critical flag When reporting an I&A, it can be defined as 'Critical'. This is then always at the top of the list of I&A, in red, when viewing all I&A.

It can only be removed from the 'Critical' list by the Safety Admin. A Critical Incident

email is generated, (over and above the existing email alerts), and is sent to people defined on the Critical Incident list.

A note is added to the data entry section that defines a Critical Incident:

A critical incident is an event posing an immediate, severe threat to health, safety,

security, or significant operational disruption, requiring urgent response.

Incidents and Accidents > Managers Ensure > I&A > Reporting Injury Forced to Escalate when an Injured Person is selected as one of the Primary

Categories.

Ensure > I&A > View, Edit, Escalate, Close > Filters When viewing reported I&A, you can now filter all Incidents and Accidents by

category.

Ensure > I&A > View existing I&A > New filters When viewing an Escalated Incident / Accident, you can now use a filter to skip between the different pages of the I&A report.

Ensure > I&A > View, Edit, Escalate, Close > Email error flag When viewing reported I&A, you will now see a red triangle next to any incident where an email error exists in the log.

Incidents and Accidents › Safety Admins

Ensure > I&A > Exporting results > New CSV KPI data When exporting Incident / Accident results, you can now select 'CSV - KPI Data' as am option. This exports just the key KPI data for any Board reporting, without all the additional data.

Ensure > I&A > Safety Admin > Request for Escalation Safety Admin can now request for a logged Incident/Accident to be escalated. Triggering an Escalation request email to the location. Once escalation requested, location admin cannot close accident. Safety Admin can also resend Request for Escalation email. In the view all I&A list, I&A are flagged as being 'Escalation

Requested'.

Ensure › I&A › Critical incident types Clients can designate certain incident types as critical by default, regardless of the

priority assigned by the person reporting the incident.

Ensure > I&A > Critical incident email Added the Title and Description of I&A to the Critical Incident email to give more headline information to recipients.

Ensure > I&A > View, Edit > Insurer reference field Managers can now enter an Insurance reference number field on the 'Initial report'

page (if a case number is subsequently provided by the insurer). The number entered will appear on the csv download of all I&A. Ensure > I&A > Email to Insurer limited to one If an existing escalated I&A is re-submitted, the insurer (if defined) will not be notified with a second email, to avoid the scenario of duplicate cases being created for the same I&A.

Ensure › I&A › Resend email to Insurer Safety Admin can choose to trigger a second Escalated I&A email to the Insurer.

Ensure > Corrective Actions > Ticket logged Managers can now mark Corrective Actions as 'Ticket logged' and enter a ticket number. Corrective Actions that have this flag are excluded from the Ensure Score.
