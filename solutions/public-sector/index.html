The operating environment

Complex structures make local ownership as important as central consistency.

Public-sector organisations often combine central policy and oversight with departments, services, regions and local sites that still own day-to-day delivery. Fit2Trade is most useful where those layers need to share the same people, site and role structure without flattening local responsibility.

Multi-level organisational hierarchy Learning across services and roles Workplace safety and recurring standards Licences, certificates and contractors where relevant
Where Fit2Trade helps

Keep central expectations connected to local responsibility.
