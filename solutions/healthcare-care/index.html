The operating environment

Workforce responsibilities are spread across services, locations and roles.

For care organisations, the Fit2Trade opportunity is around the workforce and workplace: getting required learning to the right people, keeping health and safety activity accountable, maintaining organisational or site certificates and giving employees a reliable route to current information.

Role-specific workforce learning Workplace health & safety activity Organisation and site certificates Communication across distributed teams
Where Fit2Trade helps
