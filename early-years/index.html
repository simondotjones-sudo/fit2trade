Early Years FAQs

The practical questions, answered.

Course access
Which courses are included? +

Early Years access includes 33 online courses across Data Security, Diversity & Inclusion, Food Safety, Operational H&S, Professional Development, Management Skills and Microsoft Skills. The full course list is shown above.

How do practical assessments work? +

Complete an assessment request form in the Fit2Trade portal. The request goes to our assessor partner, who will contact you directly to arrange a suitable date and time.

What do practical assessments cost? +

Manual Handling is €20 per person, Fire Safety is €20 per person and First Aid is €35 per person.

Who do we pay for a practical assessment? +

Practical assessment payment is arranged directly with the assessor partner. Fit2Trade provides the request route through the portal; you agree the timing and payment with the assessor partner.

Can we request practical assessments only when we need them? +

Yes. Submit an assessment request through the portal whenever your setting needs a Manual Handling, Fire Safety or First Aid practical assessment.

Can managers see course completion records? +

Yes. Manager oversight includes enrolment and completion visibility, with structured records of completed learning and available certificates.

How do we get started with direct Fit2Trade access? +
