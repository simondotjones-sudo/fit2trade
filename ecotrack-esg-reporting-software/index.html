Fit2Trade extension

Controlled carbon reporting without the spreadsheet sprawl.

EcoTrack is a Fit2Trade extension for organisations that need a clearer monthly view of emissions data, factors, actions and reporting boundaries. It is being shaped around a simple goal: make environmental reporting controlled, reviewable and useful to management.

One controlled dataset

Turn monthly carbon reporting into a repeatable workflow.

EcoTrack is being designed around the controls that matter when data comes from multiple sites, sources and reporting periods.

01

Activity data

Bring source data into a financial-year ledger organised by site, period and emissions source.

02

Factor control

Keep the calculation methodology and approved factor sets visible, including jurisdiction-specific factors where required.

03

Management view

Give leadership a cleaner monthly trend, source profile and data-readiness view without rebuilding the workbook each month.

04

Reporting boundaries

Use the same controlled dataset for different management and reporting views while keeping each boundary distinct.

Monthly control cycle

See the footprint, the evidence and what needs attention next.

The prototype has been shaped around a practical reporting cycle rather than a one-off annual calculation.

  1. 01
    Import

    Load activity data from the source records already used by the organisation.

  2. 02
    Review

    Surface missing records, factor issues and other exceptions instead of hiding them.

  3. 03
    Calculate

    Apply the approved methodology consistently across the agreed financial year and reporting boundary.

  4. 04
    Act

    Connect reduction actions and management follow-up to the trends that need attention.

  5. 05
    Report

    Prepare controlled management outputs from the same underlying dataset.

Clear scope

Keep source systems in place. Control the reporting layer.

Finance, travel, fleet and utility systems can remain the source of activity data. EcoTrack is being designed to control the carbon-reporting workflow around that data, rather than become another system of record for everything.

Included directionFinancial-year ledger, factors, exceptions, trends, actions and report preparation. Separately scopedIndependent assurance, formal regulatory sign-off and bespoke integrations.
