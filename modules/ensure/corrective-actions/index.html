The operational problem

Corrective actions fail when they become a separate list with no context.

Follow-up is easier to manage when the action stays connected to the checklist, incident, observation or audit finding that created it. Ensure keeps that relationship visible alongside the owner, due date and evidence.

The connective layer across Ensure

Issue → Action → Owner → Evidence → Resolution

Corrective actions provide a common follow-up path across several Ensure capabilities without losing the record that triggered the work.

  1. 01
    Issue

    A checklist, incident, observation or audit identifies something that needs attention.

  2. 02
    Action

    Define the corrective work required.

  3. 03
    Owner

    Make responsibility and the due date visible.

  4. 04
    Evidence

    Add the supporting record around the completed work.

  5. 05
    Resolve

    Close the loop while retaining the link to the original issue.
