The operational problem

Paper routines are difficult to oversee once the form leaves the site.

Recurring H&S checks only help when teams know what is due, completion can be seen and anything needing attention has a clear route into follow-up. Digital checklists keep those parts of the routine together.

A recurring routine, not another paper trail

Create → Schedule → Complete → Act → Review

The routine stays simple for site teams while giving managers a clearer view of what has been completed and what still needs action.

  1. 01
    Configure

    Set up the H&S checklist and the checks it contains.

  2. 02
    Schedule

    Set the recurring daily, weekly or monthly routine.

  3. 03
    Complete

    Site teams work through the checklist and record the result.

  4. 04
    Act

    Raise follow-up where a check identifies an issue.

  5. 05
    Review

    See completion and the action connected to the original check.

At the point of work

Capture what matters without turning the routine into administration.

01

Checklist responses

Record the required H&S checks in a consistent format.

02

Comments & photographs

Add context or supporting evidence where the checklist calls for it.

03

Issues requiring action

Keep a flagged item connected to the corrective action that follows.

For managers and safety leaders

Managers see the routine and the exceptions.

  • See whether scheduled H&S routines have been completed.
  • Focus on issues that require follow-up rather than chasing paper forms.
  • Keep corrective actions connected to the original checklist record.
