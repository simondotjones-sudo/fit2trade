The operational problem

An audit has limited value if the findings disappear into a report after the review is finished.

The service and the software need to be clear. Fit2Trade provides the H&S audit review; Ensure provides the connected safety system where findings, corrective actions and follow-up can remain visible.

Two parts of the proposition
01

Professional auditFit2Trade reviews the relevant H&S documents, evidence and safety-management activity.

02

FindingsThe audit identifies the areas requiring attention or follow-up.

03

Corrective actionsResulting actions can be managed through Ensure.

04

Follow-upKeep progress and subsequent review connected to the audit outcome.

From review to follow-up

Audit → Findings → Actions → Follow-up

The audit service provides the independent review. Ensure keeps the resulting management work connected afterwards.

For managers and safety leaders

Use the audit as a management process, not a standalone report.

  • Keep findings visible to the people responsible for follow-up.
  • Manage corrective actions in the same Ensure environment used for other safety activity.
  • Retain the audit outcome alongside the wider organisational safety record.
