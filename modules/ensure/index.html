The operational problem

Safety information is only useful when the next action is visible.

Checks, incidents, hazards and risk records can quickly become separate pieces of administration. Ensure keeps the record, responsibility and follow-up connected so issues can move through to resolution.

How Ensure works

From safety activity to accountable follow-up.

Different safety inputs can follow one clear management path, keeping the original record connected to the action that follows.

  1. 01
    Record

    Complete a safety routine or capture an incident, hazard or observation.

  2. 02
    Review

    Keep the relevant risk, audit and supporting evidence together.

  3. 03
    Act

    Assign an owner, due date and the action required.

  4. 04
    Resolve

    Track the issue and its evidence through to resolution.

Two sides of the same work

Simple for the people doing it. Visible to the people responsible.

For site teams

A consistent way to record what happened.

  • Complete scheduled safety routines.
  • Capture incidents, hazards and near misses in one flow.
  • Add the evidence needed around the work.
For safety leaders

See where follow-up is needed.

  • Keep corrective actions tied to owners and due dates.
  • Review risk assessments, audits and supporting records.
  • See safety activity across the organisation in one connected system.
