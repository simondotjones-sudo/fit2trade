The operational problem

An unsafe condition can sit between “someone noticed it” and “someone owns it.”

Hazards and safety-related maintenance issues need a route from observation into responsibility and resolution. Ensure gives frontline teams a consistent reporting point while keeping the management follow-up connected.

From observation to resolution

Identify → Report → Own → Resolve

The key is not simply collecting reports. It is making sure the issue has a visible management path afterwards.

  1. 01
    Identify

    A frontline employee notices an unsafe condition or safety-related maintenance issue.

  2. 02
    Report

    Record the issue in the appropriate Ensure reporting flow.

  3. 03
    Own

    Make responsibility for the follow-up visible.

  4. 04
    Resolve

    Track the issue and its resulting action through to closure.

  • Track safety-related maintenance concerns as part of the Ensure follow-up process.
  • Connect corrective actions where the reported condition requires a defined action.
