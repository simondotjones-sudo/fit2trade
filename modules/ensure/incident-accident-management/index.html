The operational problem

An incident record loses value when the details and the follow-up live in different places.

Inconsistent forms, separate emails and disconnected actions make it harder to understand what happened and what was done afterwards. Ensure provides one structured route from the first report into management review.

A clearer event journey

Log → Capture → Escalate → Review

The record develops as responsibility moves from the person reporting the event to the managers reviewing it.

  1. 01
    Log

    Start the incident or accident record close to where the event happened.

  2. 02
    Capture

    Record the relevant people, place, witnesses and description.

  3. 03
    Escalate

    Move the record into the appropriate management review process.

  4. 04
    Review

    Keep the event, additional detail and resulting follow-up together.
