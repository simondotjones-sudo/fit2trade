The operational problem

Close calls are easy to lose when reporting them feels harder than walking away.

A near miss needs a simple reporting route and a clear management response. Ensure keeps the close-call record available for review so the organisation can decide what action is required.

A short route from report to review

Report → Review → Act

Near-miss reporting should be quick at the frontline and useful at management level.

  2. 02
    Review

  3. 03
    Act

For managers and safety leaders

Use the report as a starting point for follow-up.

  • See near-miss reports within the wider Ensure safety record.
  • Review the event separately from incidents that caused harm.
  • Connect corrective action where the report identifies something that needs to change.
