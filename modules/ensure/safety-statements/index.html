The operational problem

A Safety Statement is difficult to manage if distribution and acknowledgement are invisible.

The document itself is only one part of the job. Organisations also need a practical way to make the relevant site statement available to employees and retain evidence that it has been read and signed.

What Ensure supports
01

Site-specific contentKeep the Safety Statement relevant to the location it covers.

02

Digital accessMake the relevant statement available to employees.

03

Read & signCapture employee acknowledgement of the document.

04

Management visibilitySee the acknowledgement record without chasing paper sign-off sheets.

From document to acknowledgement

Prepare → Distribute → Read & sign → Review

Keep the Safety Statement and employee acknowledgement inside the same connected organisational structure.

  1. 01
    Prepare

    Create or manage the Safety Statement for the relevant site.

  2. 02
    Distribute

    Make the digital statement available to the employees who need it.

  3. 03
    Read & sign

    Employees access the document and record their acknowledgement.

  4. 04
    Review

    Managers can see the acknowledgement record for the people and site concerned.
