The operational problem

A risk assessment can be complete on paper and still fail to reach the people exposed to the risk.

Ensure connects the assessment with the communication that follows. Relevant hazards and controls can be presented as Job Safety Training, giving employees a practical way to complete and acknowledge the guidance linked to their work.

From assessment to communication

Assess → Control → Communicate → Complete

Job Safety Training sits inside Ensure because it communicates the controls arising from safety risk—not because it replaces the Learn LMS.

  1. 01
    Assess

    Bring the relevant risk assessment into the safety management record.

  2. 02
    Control

    Keep the identified hazards and control measures clear.

  3. 03
    Communicate

    Present those controls through focused Job Safety Training.

  4. 04
    Complete

    Employees work through the JST and the completion record remains visible.

For managers and safety leaders

Keep the risk record and the communication around it connected.

  • Review risk assessments within the wider Ensure safety record.
  • Use JST to communicate the hazards and controls employees need to understand.
  • See completion without turning Job Safety Training into a separate general LMS.
Ensure + Learn

Job Safety Training is not a second LMS.

JST is the safety communication attached to risk: hazards, controls and the guidance employees need around the work. Broader compliance and professional learning remains in Learn ↗.
