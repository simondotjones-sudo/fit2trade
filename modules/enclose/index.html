The operational problem

Licence records become harder to control when ownership and renewal dates live in different places.

Enclose gives locations and central teams one structured register for licences, permits, certificates and supporting documents, with the responsible owner and renewal timing kept alongside the record.

How Enclose works

A controlled record from licence to renewal.

Each record keeps the licence, its supporting documentation, the responsible owner and the renewal date together so responsibility stays visible.

  1. 01
    Record

    Create a structured record for the licence, permit or certificate.

  2. 02
    Attach

    Keep the current certificate and supporting files with that record.

  3. 03
    Own

    Make the responsible site or central owner visible.

  4. 04
    Renew

    Keep upcoming expiry and renewal timing in view.

Two sides of the same work

Simple for the people doing it. Visible to the people responsible.

For site teams

Know what your location holds and what needs attention.

  • Keep local licences and supporting files in one structured place.
  • See which records are approaching renewal.
  • Keep responsibility clear for the records owned by the site.
For central teams

Maintain oversight across locations.

  • Bring organisation and location records into one register.
  • See ownership and renewal timing without separate trackers.
  • Keep current supporting documentation attached to the right record.
More than a document store

Control what is required, who owns it and what happens at renewal.

Enclose lets you define licence requirements at organisation or site level, keep the current record and owner visible, and manage the renewal cycle without relying on separate spreadsheets and folders.
