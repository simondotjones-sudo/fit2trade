The operational problem

Frontline communication needs a dependable route to the people it is meant for.

Not every employee has a company inbox, and organisation-wide messages are not always relevant to every site or role. Engage gives central teams one place to publish useful information and target it to the right audience.

How Engage works

Publish once. Target the audience. Give people one place to look.

Engage connects central communication with the same organisational structure used across Fit2Trade, so information can be directed by site, role or team.

Two sides of the same work

Simple for the people doing it. Visible to the people responsible.

For employees

One reliable place for useful company information.

  • Access news without depending on a company email address.
  • Find current benefits, policies, forms and links.
  • See internal vacancies and development opportunities.
For central teams

Put the right information in front of the right audience.

  • Publish organisation-wide or target specific groups.
  • Use the existing site, role and team structure.
  • Keep important resources in one current location rather than scattered channels.
