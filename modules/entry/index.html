The operational problem

Contractor readiness is harder to confirm when evidence is still being chased as the job approaches.

Entry brings contractor details, insurance, qualifications and site requirements into one review process so the people responsible can see what is current and who is ready to attend site.

How Entry works

Build the contractor record before the work starts.

The contractor profile, supporting documentation and site requirements can be reviewed together, with readiness and expiry status kept visible to administrators.

    Profile

    Maintain contractor company, contact and trade information.

  2. 02

  3. 03
    Review

    Check the contractor record against the relevant site requirements.

  4. 04
    Ready

    Identify who is ready to attend site and keep document expiry visible.

Two sides of the same work

Simple for the people doing it. Visible to the people responsible.

For contractors

A clear set of requirements before attending site.

  • Provide company, contact and trade information.
  • Supply insurance, qualifications and required documentation.
  • Complete the information needed for the readiness review.
For administrators

See readiness before the job begins.

  • Keep contractor profiles and requirements in one place.
  • Review documentation and readiness consistently.
  • See documents approaching expiry or already requiring replacement.
