The operational problem

Contractor compliance has two parts: the company evidence and the people doing the work.

Entry lets contractors upload the documentation they are required to hold, while clients can see which contractor employees have completed the relevant training. A clear PASS shows when both the contractor and the employee are compliant.

How Entry works

Build the compliance record before the work starts.

Contractor documentation and contractor-employee training status can be reviewed together, so clients can distinguish between missing evidence and a fully compliant contractor and worker.

  1. 01
    Upload

    The contractor supplies the company documentation required by the client.

  2. 02
    Train

    Contractor employees complete the relevant training requirements.

  3. 03
    Review

    The client reviews contractor documents and employee training status against the applicable requirements.

  4. 04
    PASS

    When both levels are compliant, the contractor and employee can be given a clear PASS.

Two sides of the same work

Simple for the people doing it. Visible to the people responsible.

For contractors

One place to provide what the client requires.

  • Upload required company documentation directly into the contractor record.
  • Keep insurance, competency and other evidence current.
  • Have contractor employees complete the relevant training requirements.
For clients

See compliance at company and employee level.

  • See which contractors hold the documentation you require.
  • See which contractor employees have completed the relevant training.
  • Use a clear PASS to identify when both levels are compliant.
Compliance at two levels

A PASS only means something when the company and the person both meet the requirement.

Entry keeps the contractor company record and the contractor employee's training status visible as separate parts of the same compliance decision.
