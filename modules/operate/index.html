The operational problem

Standards only work when they show up in the working day.

Opening, closing, brand and quality routines need to be completed consistently across locations. Operate turns those standards into practical checks, gives teams a route to report issues and keeps follow-up visible.

A day in Operate

Standards move from central expectation to site action.

Teams complete the routine, record exceptions and keep the issue attached to the work, while operational leaders retain visibility across locations.

  1. 01
    Set the routine

    Turn operational standards into checklists for the work that needs to happen.

  2. 02
    Complete the work

    Sites work through opening, closing, quality and other operational checks.

  3. 03
    Record the issue

    Capture a problem with the relevant comment, photo or timestamp where needed.

  4. 04
    Follow through

    Assign the issue and keep completion patterns visible across sites.

Two sides of the same work

Simple for the people doing it. Visible to the people responsible.

For site teams

Simple routines built around the work.

  • Use clear checklists for recurring operational activity.
  • Record an issue at the point it is found.
  • Keep supporting comments, photos and timestamps with the work.
For operational leaders

A clearer view across locations.

  • Compare completion patterns between sites.
  • See recurring gaps and location-level performance.
  • Follow issues through to closure without separate manual reports.
Built around real operational routines

Use the same workflow for the standards that matter to your business.

Operate is not limited to opening and closing checks. The same practical checklist and follow-up model can support recurring standards, changeovers, controls and management visits across locations.

Site & regional visits
