The operational problem

Get the right learning to the right people without losing sight of progress.

Required learning changes by role, site and team. Learn keeps delivery and completion visibility together so people know what they need to do and managers can see what is still outstanding.

How Learn works

A clear path from learning need to visible completion.

Learning is assigned through the organisation structure, accessed by the learner and kept visible to the people responsible for completion.

Two sides of the same work

Simple for the people doing it. Visible to the people responsible.

For learners

Clear learning, in one familiar place.

  • See the learning relevant to their role.
  • Access bite-sized, mobile-ready content.
  • Move through required and development learning from one account.
For managers

Know what is complete and what needs attention.

  • Assign learning to the right groups.
  • See progress and overdue learning without manual chasing.
  • Keep certification status and learning records visible.
More than course delivery

Build capability and keep the learning record around it visible.

Learn can combine structured learning paths with assessments, certificates, expiry dates, practical sign-off and manager reporting, alongside your own organisation content.
